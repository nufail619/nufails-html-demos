ABOUT MAYA

Good design starts with good questions.

I’m Maya Rowan, a fictional product designer who enjoys making complicated systems feel more approachable.

A little about my approach

Make space for clarity.

I work with teams that have a meaningful idea and a lot of moving parts. Together, we find the real problem, listen to the people affected, and shape the simplest useful path forward.

I believe strong design can be both ambitious and practical: clear enough to use, flexible enough to grow, and thoughtful enough to feel human.

Tools I work with

A flexible design toolkit.

  • Interface sketching and wireframing
  • Interactive prototyping
  • Research synthesis boards
  • Component and pattern libraries
  • Collaborative planning documents

Learning & values

Curious by default.

Education: Bachelor of Design, fictional program in Digital Experience Design.

Values: thoughtful listening, accessible choices, shared ownership, and steady iteration.

My goal is to leave a team with clearer decisions and a system they can keep building on.
