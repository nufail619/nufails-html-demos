Frequently asked questions

A few things to know before you reach out.

What happens during an initial consultation?

It is a chance to outline your question, share relevant context, and discuss possible next steps. This sample website does not provide legal advice.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Bring any documents that help explain your question. The team can let you know if anything else would be useful.

Do you work with individuals and businesses?

This fictional firm website describes sample practice areas for both individuals and organizations.

How are fees discussed?

Scope and potential fees are discussed before work begins, based on the matter and applicable arrangements.
